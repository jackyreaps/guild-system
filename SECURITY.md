# Security Policy

## Scope

This repository contains policy documents. It does not contain runtime code, keys, member data, or live ledgers. Security issues in this repository are therefore about the **integrity and confidentiality of the policy documents themselves**, and about the **safety of members and guilds named in or affected by those documents**.

## What to report

Report the following to the contact below:

- A policy document that has been tampered with, defaced, or replaced.
- A vulnerability in the policy that enables capture, coercion, or deanonymization of members.
- A predatory legal demand, hostile-state action, or coordinated attack against a member or guild.
- A leak of member PII or legal identity through this repository.
- A broken or malicious link in a policy document that redirects to a hostile resource.

Do **not** open a public issue for any of the above. Public disclosure before mitigation can endanger members.
