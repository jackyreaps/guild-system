# Local Guild Charter — org:guild:demo

A minimal example of a filed Local Guild Charter for a fictional guild, demonstrating the required sections of GS-CHR-010 §2. This file is illustrative. It is not a real charter. Do not adopt it.

## 1. Guild Identity

- **Name.** Demo Guild
- **Guild ID.** `demo`
- **Founding date.** 2026-11-04
- **Founding members.** `alice`, `bob`

## 2. Jurisdictions

- **Operation.** Switzerland, Singapore
- **Asset holding.** Switzerland, Singapore, Iceland
- **Member residence (aggregated).** Europe, Asia-Pacific

## 3. Legal Form

- **Entity type.** Swiss Stiftung (foundation)
- **Registration jurisdiction.** Switzerland (Zug)
- **Registered agent.** Demo Treuhand AG (fictional)

## 4. Membership and Alias Rules

- **Join process.** Application with two peer attestations.
- **Membership type.** Application.
- **Alias policy.** Monikers are persistent. Cross-guild linkage is optional.

## 5. Internal Governance

- **Decision process.** Rough consensus with a rotating steward.
- **Term limits.** Steward serves 6 months, not renewable consecutively.
- **Amendment process.** Two-thirds of members; 14-day comment.

## 6. Currency and Exchange

- **System.** Closed-loop Credits (GS-CHR-003 default).
- **Compliance annex.** None required (closed-loop).

## 7. Merit Recognition

- **Calibration.** Per-guild, subject to cross-branch peer attestation.
- **Public-good additions.** Local science literacy program.

## 8. Contribution Verification

- **Methods.** `independent_rerun`, `proof_checker`, `independent_replication`, `two_member_peer_attestation` (for command contribution).

## 9. Founding Orientation

- **Statement.** Preserve the ability of independent researchers to audit training pipelines without requiring institutional affiliation.
- **Field coupled to.** Public-interest AI safety research.
- **Invariants.**
  1. Artifacts are published, not hoarded.
  2. No single funder above one-third of income.
  3. No direct sovereign counterparty.
  4. Fork is always available.

## 10. Shear Detection Method

- **Method.** Hybrid.
- **Indicators.**
  - `funding_concentration`
  - `jurisdiction_concentration`
  - `artifact_drift`
- **Threshold.** Any indicator above the published threshold for two consecutive review cycles.
- **Review cadence.** Quarterly.

## 11. Fork Conditions

Members may fork to preserve the founding orientation if:

1. The founding orientation is materially changed without the §5 amendment process.
2. A single funder exceeds one-third of income for two consecutive quarters.
3. The governance process is suspended or bypassed for more than 30 days.

## 12. Guardrail Declaration

The guild complies with all seven drift pattern prohibitions (GS-CHR-005 §2). No exceptions.

## 13. Conflict-of-Interest Rules

- **Disclosure.** Members in governance roles disclose financial relationships with funders, counterparties, and attestees.
- **Recusal.** Disclosed conflicts require recusal from the relevant decision.

## 14. Legal Compliance Statement

- **Tax-Integrity Floor.** The guild meets all lawful tax obligations in Switzerland and Singapore. No structure conceals income or defeats a lawful obligation.
- **Disclosure boundary.** Beneficial ownership is available on lawful request at every layer (GS-OPS-005 §6).
