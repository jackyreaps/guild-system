# GS-CHR-001 — Hostile-State Resilience Charter

**Adversarial legality doctrine for a polycentric network operating in a world where law is often the weapon.**

## 1. Purpose

The Guild System operates across jurisdictions that range from strong rule-of-law states to failed states with no functioning legal system, to captured states where law is written to serve predation, to states that pass facially valid laws designed to target public-interest work specifically.

This Charter establishes the doctrine for operating under those conditions. It does not assume that law is a neutral referee. In many jurisdictions, the law is the instrument.

The doctrine is defensive and human-rights grounded. It is not a license for covert action. Every provision assumes the Compact's Operating Article I (public or authorized data only) and the disclosure boundary (`GS-OPS-005` §6).

## 2. The Three Realities the Charter Assumes

**2.1 Not all places have functioning law.** Some jurisdictions have no functioning legal system — civil war, state collapse, or de facto rule by armed groups. In these places, compliance with "the law" is not a coherent category because there is no coherent law to comply with. The doctrine there is protection, not procedure.

**2.2 Some places have systematically corrupt law.** Captured judiciaries, bought legislators, and regulatory agencies that serve the regulated rather than the public. In these places, a law that is procedurally valid on its face may be substantively predatory. Following due process is not the same as being just.

**2.3 Some places use law retroactively or selectively as a weapon.** Targeted legislation that names a specific organization. Retroactive liability applied to conduct that was lawful when performed. Procedural traps designed to exhaust rather than to adjudicate. The legal process itself becomes the punishment.

**The Guild System's defense is not "we followed the law."** Its defense is that its work is public, verifiable, and serves the public interest — and that the world can see how it is being treated. Transparency and international political cost are the actual defenses. Compliance is conditional, not automatic.

## 3. Radical Legal Integrity

### 3.1 The Armor of Transparency

The Guild System prohibits covert data acquisition, unauthorized access, and criminal hacking. Public or explicitly authorized data only. This is both ethical commitment and legal armor: verifiable public evidence is the only evidence that survives scrutiny without creating liability.

### 3.2 Law as Weapon

A demand can be lawful under local statute and still be predatory. The Legitimacy Test (`GS-CHR-006` §6) assesses every demand against seven criteria: lawful basis, due process, proportionality, jurisdiction, human-rights compliance, targeting of lawful work, absence of pretext.

A demand that satisfies local statute but fails the test — for example, a retroactive law targeting public-interest work, or a procedurally valid prosecution of a researcher whose work the state finds inconvenient — is a Predatory Request. It is documented, challenged through lawful channels where they exist, and publicized where they do not.

### 3.3 The Legitimacy Test in Practice

The Juridical Chamber (`GS-CHR-006`) classifies every demand on the Demand Determination Scale. The scale ranges from full compliance to resist. No classification is automatic. The presence of a statute is a starting point, not an answer.

The Chamber's classifications are published. If a state disagrees, the record is public.

### 3.4 Human Rights Floor

Anchored in the UDHR, ICCPR, UNESCO open-science principles, responsible disclosure norms, and academic freedom standards. These do not override local law. They guide the moral and narrative frame across borders, and they inform the Legitimacy Test.

## 4. Polycentric Continuity

**4.1 No Central Point of Failure.** A polycentric network of Circles. If one is compromised, seized, or enjoined, it is pruned via revocation protocols. Other Circles route around the disruption.

**4.2 Compartmentalized Knowledge.** No member is required or allowed to store information about the physical location or legal identity of other members. Knowledge is distributed on a need-to-know basis.

**4.3 Epistemic Firebreak.** A compromised individual cannot be forced to compromise the network at large because they do not possess the network's full map.

## 5. Collective Human Defense

**5.1 Sovereign Relocation Protocol.** A dedicated fraction of lawful funding streams is held in compliant escrow for emergency asylum, physical relocation, and legal defense.

**5.2 Emergency Legal Fund.** Mutual aid for members facing retaliation, prosecution, or civil suits for lawful public-interest work. Transparent, charter-governed, counsel-approved. Administered by `GS-CHR-006` §5.4.

**5.3 Mutual Defense Agreements.** Circles support each other through amicus briefs, regulatory comments, public statements, and legal coordination.

## 6. Epistemic Humility and Anti-Capture

**6.1 Open Peer Review.** All findings are submitted to open, global peer review. Authority rests on whether an independent third party can run the code and verify the math.

**6.2 Right of Reply.** Subjects of Guild critiques are offered a chance to respond. Corrections are published.

**6.3 Transparency Reports.** Legal demands, takedowns, and state pressure are published, subject to legal advice and member safety.

**6.4 Conflict-of-Interest Rules.** Funding, employment, and affiliations are disclosed where possible. No single funder can capture the Guild System.

## 7. Definitions

- **Hostile State.** A state actor using unlawful, predatory, or extrajudicial means to suppress lawful public-interest work. Includes states with no functioning legal system, states with systematically corrupt law, and states using law retroactively or selectively as a weapon.
- **Predatory Request.** A demand failing any criterion of the Legitimacy Test (`GS-CHR-006` §6).
- **Legitimate Legal Demand.** A request that satisfies the Legitimacy Test. A demand can be lawful under local statute and still fail the test.
