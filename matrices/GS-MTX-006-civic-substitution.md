# GS-MTX-006 — Civic Substitution Matrix

| Public-Good Function | State Failure Mode | Guild Substitute | Legal Basis | Tax Treatment | Prohibited Version |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Education | Underfunded schools | Study circles, tutoring, courses | Educational non-profit, cooperative | Credits non-realized | Disguised wages |
| Health / mental health | Access gaps, cost barriers | Peer support, care mutuals | Mutual benefit society | Usually no realization | Unlicensed medical practice |
| Local infrastructure | Neglect, corruption | Tool libraries, repair co-ops | Cooperative law | Non-convertible credits | Private capture of public assets |
| Compute / data | Corporate enclosure | Open compute co-ops, public datasets | Non-profit, cooperative | Barter contract, reported | Unreported barter |
| Emergency response | Slow or absent | Local mutual aid networks | Mutual-aid statutes | Donation-based | Self-dealing |
| Legal aid | Cost-prohibitive | Rights education, counsel networks | Non-profit legal clinic | Pro bono | Unauthorized practice of law |
| Science / literacy | Politicized or defunded | Public research, open courses | Educational non-profit | Non-realized | Grant fraud |
| Care work | Unpaid or unaffordable | Care cooperatives, time banks | Time-bank / LETS | Non-realized | Wage-law avoidance |
| Elder / child support | Inadequate safety net | Mutual childcare, elder circles | Fraternal society law | Non-realized | Concealed employment |
