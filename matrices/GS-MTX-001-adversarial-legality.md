# GS-MTX-001 — Adversarial Legality Matrix

| Hostile State Vector | Threat / Mechanism | Guild Defense Layer | Operational Reality | Legal Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Judicial weaponization | Fabricated charges, SLAPPs, asset freezing, abusive injunctions | Jurisdiction diversity, Emergency Legal Fund, counsel network | Entities, treasuries, repositories across strong rule-of-law jurisdictions. Compliant escrow releases defense funds to local counsel upon Chamber approval. | No evasion of valid court orders. Unlawful orders are challenged and documented. |
| Infiltration | Informants, coerced insiders, social-graph mapping | Zero-knowledge persona decoupling, need-to-know compartmentalization | No master roster. Infiltration yields only an isolated Forge Circle. Trust via signed artifacts. | No covert data acquisition. Conflicts disclosed where required. |
| Surveillance | Metadata collection, device compromise, censorship | Metadata minimization, E2E encryption, air-gapped signing | Privacy-preserving tools consistent with local law. Reproducible builds let anyone verify. | Comply with lawful intercept; challenge unlawful surveillance. |
| Extradition / travel bans | Interpol abuse, arbitrary detention | Human Rights Escrow, NGO and UN channels | Pre-staged advocacy with UN Special Rapporteurs, Amnesty, Article 19. Legal observers, asylum/relocation fund. | No evasion of valid extradition. Unlawful notices contested lawfully. |
| Financial exclusion | Banking blocks, sanctions abuse, payment deplatforming | Diversified lawful treasury, compliance review, mutual aid | Multi-jurisdiction banking with AML/KYC. Emergency fund for retaliation. | Full compliance. No sanctions evasion. |
| Platform censorship | Deplatforming, DNS blocks, account bans | Mirrors, content-addressed storage, open protocols | Artifacts on multiple independent mirrors. Distribution Circles maintain redundancy. | Comply with lawful takedowns; publish transparency reports; contest overbroad orders. |
| Disinformation / reputation | Smears, forged documents, coordinated harassment | Signed artifacts, transparency logs, right of reply | All outputs cryptographically signed and independently verifiable. Corrections and right-of-reply published. | Defamation law applies. Corrections published promptly. |
| Physical threats | Detention, violence, threats to family | Relocation protocol, mutual defense, emergency legal fund | Emergency asylum, relocation, legal defense pool. Family protection in risk assessment. | Lawful asylum and human-rights mechanisms. No retaliation or violence. |
| Co-option | Grants, buyouts, capture by funders | Polycentric governance, anti-capture bylaws, conflict-of-interest rules | No central entity to buy. Multiple lawful entities with separated powers. Funding transparency. | Lawful incorporation with governance safeguards. |
| Internal capture | Ego, factionalism, mission drift | Rough consensus, running code, rotating roles, ethics council | Decisions require math and code, not charisma. Ombuds and ethics council. | Internal arbitration does not override courts. |
| Law as weapon | Facially valid statute written to target public-interest work; retroactive liability; procedural traps | Legitimacy Test, Demand Determination Scale, transparency | Chamber classifies every demand against seven criteria. Public record of classifications. | Non-compliance with predatory demands through lawful channels; documentation and publication. |
