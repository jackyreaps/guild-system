# GS-MTX-005 — Guild Credit Exposure Matrix

| Use Case | Convertibility | Legal Regime Triggered | Lawful Mitigation | Prohibited Version |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Internal compute purchase | Closed-loop | Generally none | Non-transferable unit | — |
| Learning bounty in credits | Closed-loop | Low | Non-redeemable, internal only | Cash payout disguised as credits |
| Mutual credit for review labor | Mutual | Low–moderate | Time-bank precedent, documented | Off-book wage substitution |
| Stipend for living costs | Semi-closed | Income tax likely | Disclose, withhold if required | Concealed compensation |
| Inter-guild settlement | Mutual | Varies | Counsel per jurisdiction | Unlicensed transmission |
| External redemption | Convertible | Full regime | Licensed rails, AML/KYC | Anonymous cash-out |
| Compute barter | Barter | Often taxable | Contract, report | Unreported barter |
| Mutual aid pool | Non-convertible | Usually none | Non-profit structure | Tax-evasion vehicle |
